1 About this Book

Book Overview & Supplementary Materials

Robust Portfolio Construction
and Risk Analysis

Taming Outliers and Reducing Estimation Error.

To be published by Springer in 2026

R. Douglas Martin  ·  Thomas K. Philips  ·  Bernd Scherer  ·  Kirk Li

Robust Portfolio Construction and Risk Analysis (RPCRA) is a graduate level textbook that provides a comprehensive and rigorous account of the mathematical foundations of modern portfolio theory viewed through the immensely practical lens of Robust Statistical Methods. The book develops the mathematical, statistical, and computational tools and techniques needed to construct, evaluate, and manage investment portfolios in the presence of noise.

The text integrates classical mean-variance portfolio theory — the estimation of expected returns and modeling risk, cross-section and time-series factor models, the construction of optimal portfolios and the measurement of their performance — with modern statistical methodology including robust estimators that are not much influenced by outliers, influence function-based analyses of risk and performance estimators, and statistical process control-based monitoring of portfolio performance.

These techiques are applied to active equity and fixed income portfolios, as well as to the construction of specialized portfolios that are of great practical importance: miminimum downside risk portfolios, portfolios designed to meet spending objectives, and portfolios with options. Throughout, the authors emphasize the computational aspects of portfolio management and make available the companion open-source PCRA package to reproduce many of the exhibits in the book as well as for non-commercial use by readers.

2 Preface
