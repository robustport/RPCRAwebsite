About

Authors, Book Information, and Contact

1 The Book

Robust Portfolio Construction and Risk Analysis (RPCRA) is a graduate-level textbook providing a rigorous, unified treatment of quantitative portfolio management techniques that are designed to work in the presence of noise.

The book integrates the classical theory of mean-variance optimization, the Capital Asset Pricing Model, and the Arbitrage Pricing Theory with modern statistical methodology — including robust estimation, regularized regression, time-series factor models, and machine learning — all grounded in practical portfolio construction.

2 The Authors

R. Douglas Martin

Doug Martin is Professor Emeritus in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Washington and a founder of the Computational Finance and Risk Managment (CFRM) program. He co-founded two software firms - Statistical Sciences Inc (the S+ company) and Finanalytica, and was involved in porting the S+ robust package to R. In addition, he has co-authored two books: Modern Portfolio Optimization (with Bernd Scherer) and Robust Statistics : Theory and Methods (with Ricardo Maronna, Victor Yohai and Matias Salibian-Barrera).

Thomas K. Philips

Tom Philips is an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Finance and Risk Engineering at NYU’s Tandon School of Engineering. Prior to teaching Portfolio Managment and Corporate Valuation Theory at Tandon, he was the Global Head of Front Office Risk for the Institutional Division of BNP Paribas Investment Partners. He was awarded the First Bernstein/Fabozzi/Jacobs-Levy Prize for his paper Why Do Valuation Ratios Forecast Long-Run Equity Returns? and the Graham and Dodd Scroll Award for Saving Social Security: A Better Approach which was co-authored with Arun Muralidhar.

Bernd Scherer

Bernd Scherer is the Head of Portfolio Implementation at the Abu Dhabi Investment Authorty, and is on the Advisory Board of EDHEC’s Ph.D. in Finance program. He co-authored Portfolio Construction and Risk Budgeting with Doug Martin.

Kirk Li

Kirk Li is a Principal Machine Learning Science Manager at Microsoft. He earned his Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Washington.
